# Viva Voce Preparation & Academic Defense: Master Overview

Welcome to the Viva Voce & Presentation Preparation section of the **Adaptive Inter-Process Communication (A-IPC)** framework.

This directory is designed specifically to prepare students, researchers, and engineers to confidently explain and defend the project in front of professors, external examiners, or technical evaluators.

---

## Viva Preparation Directory

1. [Master Questions & Answers (30 Questions)](questions-and-answers.md)  
   Thirty detailed project-specific viva questions organized by difficulty (Beginner, Intermediate, and Challenging/Hostile Examiner). Every answer follows a structured pedagogical format: Direct Answer, Technical Reasoning, Implementation Pointer, Honest Limitation, and Follow-up Question.

2. [Phase-Wise FAQ](phase-wise-faq.md)  
   Consolidated FAQ covering the entire engineering progression from Phase 1 through Phase 6, addressing phase-specific implementation decisions.

3. [Research Defense Strategy](research-defense.md)  
   High-level defense guidelines for handling hostile academic challenges regarding novelty, selection overhead, comparison fairness, and hardware dependencies.

4. [Presentation Scripts & Pitch Deck](presentation-script.md)  
   Carefully rehearsed scripts for different presentation formats: a 30-second elevator pitch, a 2-minute executive summary, and a complete 10-minute capstone defense slide script.
